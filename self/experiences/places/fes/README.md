# Fes

## Morocco

### 2026-05-21

No sleep the night before — the excitement for the trip doesn't let me. Mahmoud helps with the web check-in.

#### 0320

Cab to the bus station.

#### 0400

Bus to Stansted airport. We're 35 minutes early. It's cold, barefoot dogs wandering about, waiting in the quiet.

Sleep through the flight, as usual. Woken by the thud of landing.

#### 0945

Land at Fes airport. Step out and am immediately hit by the Moroccan sun. Do the formalities and get out. Take a cab to the hostel for 120 MAD — seems a lot, but we go for it.

#### 1130

Arrive at the plaza in front of **Bab Boujloud** and get my first real look at a Moroccan café — full of men. Café Granada. Meet **Naseem** at the rooftop. Friendly guy. Check-in isn't until 15:00, so we drop our bags.

#### 1230

Head into the medina through Bab Boujloud — the Blue Gate, blue on the inside, green on the outside. It feels like a whole new world out there.

Walk to **Café Clock**. I have the *poulet r'fissa*; Mahmoud has the lamb tajine.

#### 1315

Deeper into the medina. Too much to take in all at once:

- Handcarts piled with garlic, snails
- Cats everywhere
- Smell of mint leaves
- Butcher shop smells
- Mules weaving through the lanes
- Tourists dragging their suitcases

Pass through **Souk el Henna**, then **Place Seffarine** — the copper souk, full of metalworkers hammering away. Then come across the Kissaria — the covered souk of fine fabrics and caftans, near the **Moulay Idriss II Mausoleum**.

Can tell we're near the tanneries before we even see them — the stench gives it away. The **Chouara Tannery**. Quite a sight.

Have fresh orange juice and *corne de gazelle aux amandes* on the spot. Nice.

Head back — navigating out is like being inside a maze.

#### 1545

Back at the hostel. Check in and sleep so well.

#### 1930

Wake and head up to the rooftop — still hot out.

Mahmoud isn't feeling well. Suggest he vomit and get it out of his system; after that he feels a lot better. Almost got the Moroccan belly.

Lounge on the rooftop drinking mint tea as Naseem keeps bringing round after round.

Go back to bed — tomorrow: day trip to [Chefchaouen](../chefchaouen).

# Chefchaouen

## Morocco

### 2026-05-22

#### 0650

Wake up late and have a quick shower. Sneak two pain au chocolat from the kitchen on the rooftop — Naseem had said feel free to help ourselves. The views from the rooftop are great, I can see all of Fes.

#### 0750

Arrive at the boutique hostel to get on the bus. Make a quick stop to get some pain au raisin. Then head to Chefchaouen, cramped in the front seat of the van. The views are pretty on the road — people carrying their stuff on mules in the heat, the shepherd and the like.

#### 1015

Stop for a break and have a cappuccino and the pain au raisin. The café has a different taste in a good way. Continue to Chefchaouen.

#### 1150

Reach Chefchaouen. The van stops to get a panoramic view.

#### 1200

Reach the medina and exchange currency, then wander through the streets to **Plaza Uta d'Hammam**, with the **Grande Mosquée** at its corner.

#### 1300

Mahmoud goes to pray. By the time it's done it takes too long — I go to get lost in the Chefchaouen streets.

#### 1310

We go to **La Fuente** for lunch. The person who guides us in insists it's the place where the locals eat. And boy did we eat like locals. The food is very budget-friendly — tagine prune et agneau, poulet couscous, and a fromage salad, but light enough to start us with zaalouk (Mahmoud notes that zaalouk means "characterless" in Egyptian Arabic — fitting, since the vegetables are churned until they lose their character). Crème caramel for dessert, followed by mint tea. As much as I love mint tea, I didn't want the mint to overpower the dessert's taste in my mouth — but it's rude not to. I gulped it and it helped me power through the climb towards the Bouzafer Mosque.

The bill arrived written on a small note, decorated in a pretty way by the girl serving us.

#### 1510

With just 30 min left and yet to see the Bouzafer Mosque and the waterfall, we hurry the climb. The stream is beautiful — locals enjoying a bath, some washing their clothes, orange produce tumbling in the falls. We tackle the climb to reach the mosque and start heading downhill. We make it 3/4 of the way and with 30 min left head back to the centre. Going downhill we go through the "hilly" section of Chefchaouen — not able to enjoy it as leisurely as I'd like. As we cross the bridge we see peacocks, and they stand well against the blue of Chefchaouen.

We make it back to the centre. Mahmoud goes to exchange currency while I selfishly get the better seat in the van and pick up my book to read.

#### 1630

Quick stop — have the jus d'orange and continue back to Fes.

#### 1940

Arrive at the Blue Gate and get down. Mahmoud got separated getting off and the driver gives him an earful — more of an angst since the driver received very few tips.

#### 2000

Back in the hostel, ask Naseem for the best pastilla.

#### 2130

Go to **Café Cinema** and have the pastilla and baba ghanouj.

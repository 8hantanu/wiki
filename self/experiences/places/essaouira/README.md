# Essaouira

## Morocco

### 2026-05-23

Continued from [Marrakech](../marrakech).

#### 2015

Reach Essaouira and can see the sun starting to set in the distance. Head towards the old town. As we enter the medina's gate it is a surreal experience — very dream-like, the haze moving through the streets with the backdrop of buildings with blue accents. There are live music performers playing and the smell of sweet sugar and flour fills the air, sea gulls squawking behind.

Riad Azul is a pretty riad. We rest there until we decide to head out for food.

#### 2200

Head out. In the street there is a tiny oven making some cookie-like things, the air filled with its sweet smell. We walk around and Mahmoud has some crepe.

#### 2220

Walk further and find the best place to hear the gnawa music — **Café des Arts**. A tiny setup with three players: the guembri, the qraqeb, and the tbel. They create such a beautiful atmosphere. It is someone's birthday too — we have some cake and cookies.

It is the last night. We head back satisfied — the perfect blue table moment. I have tabbouleh to keep it light. A candle-lit dinner.

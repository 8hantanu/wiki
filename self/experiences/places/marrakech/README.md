# Marrakech

## Morocco

### 2026-05-23

#### 0715

Wake up lazily and prepare to leave Fes. Naseem has arranged a packed breakfast left at our door — pain au chocolat, sandwiches, boiled eggs, yogurt, etc.

#### 0800

Take a petit taxi to the train station. Have a café crème macchiato at the station with the pain au chocolat Naseem packed for us.

#### 0820

Get on the train. Seats are very comfortable. Stroll outside on the platform until the train leaves right on time.

#### 0845

The views are pretty — plains in the foreground, mountains in the back. There is a pretty girl continuously trying to make eye contact, but I avoid.

#### 0945

Pass the oued Rdom, which has a vibrant flower plantation along it.

#### 0955

Orange trees at the gare de Sidi Kacem.

#### 1030

Have the sandwich Naseem packed for us. One thing I notice — hydration is contagious, like a yawn. I pick up my bottle to drink and the old lady beside me does the same, and so do 3 other people. Be the healthy catalyst to start the chain reaction to hydrate — it can win for everyone.

#### ~1130

Pass through Rabat. The Hassan Tower and the white dome of the Mausoleum of Mohammed V are visible from the window as we roll through.

#### ~1215

Pass through Casablanca.

#### 1300

Start reading and doze off to sleep.

#### 1400

Suddenly awoken by the heat of the sun on my face — out the window is the wild open desert, passing along the Oum Er-Rbia river.

#### 1545

Arrive at Marrakech. Get the currency exchanged and head out to have some lunch (more like breakfast 2.0) at **Chez Llhaj**. We have the *ftour beldi* — both the *ftour beldi* and the *ftour chamali*, the difference being the jben (goat's cheese) in the northern one. The salade assortie comes with six salads — zaalouk, taktouka, carottes caramalisées, salade marocaine, lentilles, and haricots.

Quickly eat as we have to get back to catch our bus to Essaouira.

#### 1700

Get the front view seats for the ride.

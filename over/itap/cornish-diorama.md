# The Cornish Diorama
**2026-09-01**

I was experimenting with [Ricoh's](../matter/things/camera#ricoh-gr-iiix-hdf) film simulations to get a pastel, painting-like look (quite happy with it). Since the weather kept shifting, I had to update the recipe along the way, but it was mostly a combination of high saturation and low contrast.

Here is the *Cornish Diorama* from my trip to Cornwall in the summer of 2026.
